# Hockey Drills & Games

A free drill library and AI drill generator for field hockey coaches, in Dutch, French and English. By Maroussia Walckiers.

**Open the page:** https://maroucool.github.io/hockey-drills/

## What is in here

| File | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `index.html` | The drill library: 52 drills and small games with pitch drawings, a search box and a print button. Works for everyone, no account. It also has an "AI generator" tab. |
| `own-copy.html` | Instructions, in three languages, for setting up your own one-click generator inside Claude. |
| `generator.html` | The generator on its own. This is the file a coach gives to their own Claude. |
| `manifest.webmanifest`, `sw.js`, `icon-*.png` | What lets a phone or computer install the page as an app and open it without internet. |

## Three ways to use it

1. **Library.** Open the page, pick a drill, print it.
2. **AI generator with any Claude account.** On the "AI generator" tab, fill in your group and press "Open in Claude". Send the request in your own Claude, copy the answer and paste it back on the page. The drill appears with its pitch drawing.
3. **Your own one-click copy in Claude.** Follow the steps on `own-copy.html`. After a two-minute setup, drills appear on your own copy of the page in one click, using your own Claude account.

## Use it like an app

Open the page on your phone and add it to your home screen (on iPhone: Share, then Add to Home Screen; on Android: the "Install as app" button or the browser menu). It then opens full screen from its own icon, and the library also works without internet, which is handy at the pitch.

## Good to know

- Drills from the AI generator are written by AI. Read each one before training and adapt it to your players and your club's safety rules.
- Nothing is collected about you. Recent drills are kept only in your own browser.
- Please report anything that does not work.
